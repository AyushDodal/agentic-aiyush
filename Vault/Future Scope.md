## After Recruiter Feedback

1. Add explicit owner-approved project writeups and hobbies as sources, with versioning and provenance.
2. Add an authenticated document management surface with preview and approval before indexing.
3. Create an evaluation set of factual questions, unsupported questions, follow-ups, and instruction-injection attempts.
4. Improve expression quality with a rigged custom avatar, phoneme timing, and interruption support.
5. Consider streaming responses or a realtime voice session once the accuracy and source behavior are reliable.
6. Add multilingual conversations with the same resume grounding.
7. Add aggregate usage/latency feedback only with an explicit data retention policy.

## Beyond The Resume

A broader digital persona would require explicit, owner-approved sources and clear boundaries around private experiences and sensitive information. Do not infer personality, opinions, personal stories, hobbies, or availability from the resume alone. The current scope stays professional and evidence-based.
